## What is it?

Imagine you are playing any truck simulator and you need to press the hazard indicator or turn the retarder on, you would have to press on a key of your keyboard or your steering wheel. That breaks the "simulation" part.
With TruckDeck Lite you have a truck-functions panel right in front of you ready to use at any time.

## Why?

I created this project because I had for some long time, the idea of making a hardware project. Before this I only made software projects, and some hardware projects for school, but never for fun.

This way I could be a proud owner of my own hardware project, I could even make a couple of ones to give to friends and that would mean so much to me.

I came to the idea of making TruckDeck because:
- Truck-simulators are played by most people
- It is as simple as a box with buttons (or not, but more on that later on)
- People would actually need or want my product, it isn't just some toy or crap.

### I used AI

For the creation of this project, AI was used.
I used a AI assistent (Deepseek and Google AI-mode search) for:
- Learning, I am very new to making hardware so there is soo much for me to learn
- Doing research on components (which ones to use, how they work)
- Giving me instructions about how wiring works and making + explaining the pinmap
